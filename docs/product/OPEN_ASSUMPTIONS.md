# Cleara Open Assumptions

Status: `OPEN / HYPOTHESIS`

1. The first paying/owning user is a facility sponsor or treasury operator, not a capital provider directly.
2. The reciprocal obligation pair is already finalized before the primary Cleara workflow begins.
3. The relevant source-chain asset and settlement adapter are available and authorized.
4. Institutional users can supply or delegate the required source-wallet and Creditcoin roles.
5. Legal, custody, KYC/KYB/AML, sanctions, and ERP controls remain external prerequisites.
6. Operators need investigation and evidence tooling but must not receive authority to mark state settled.
7. The current M8–M11 evidence is representative enough to validate the workflow shape, although not production evidence.
8. Clearing before settlement produces sufficient economic value to justify cross-domain proof and reconciliation overhead.
9. A sponsor will accept a composite local demonstration while demanding continuous evidence in production.
10. The facility/capital-provider lifecycle should remain a supporting relationship rather than the primary product entry point.
11. Future multi-chain expansion will be capability-specific and not presented as interchangeable chain support.
12. M15 is production frontend maturity; it is not a statement that no local research workbench may exist.
13. User identity, role binding, and session design are not finalized by the current protocol repository.
14. The exact operational SLA for source finality and proof arrival is not yet accepted.
15. The product owner accepts the clear-before-settlement recommendation before new UI work begins.
