# Cleara Claims and Limits

## Supported claims

Cleara can claim, on testnet and within the separately evidenced slices:

- Attestcoin-backed source claim ingestion;
- bounded financeability and encumbrance;
- facility and provider allocation coordination;
- source-chain capital commitments recognized on Creditcoin;
- multiparty capitalization sealed into an immutable composition;
- finalized obligations;
- explicitly authorized bilateral clearing;
- canonical residual routing;
- current-head M11 mock-token settlement with independent payer validation, Attestcoin proof, and Creditcoin reconciliation;
- current-head M11-Lifecycle consume/expire synchronization with terminal accounting reconciliation;
- a local fixture/read-model workbench that exposes provenance and uncertainty.

## Explicit limits

This submission does not claim:

- one uninterrupted M3→M11 deployment lifecycle;
- production indexer or RPC backfill;
- durable workers or durable checkpoints;
- production API or hosted frontend;
- mainnet deployment;
- production-value settlement;
- legal enforceability;
- custody, KYC/KYB, AML, sanctions, or accounting compliance;
- production stablecoin or real-asset settlement;
- Attestcoin writability or Inbox/Outbox delivery;
- universal bridge behavior;
- consumer wallet functionality;
- live writes from the UI;
- indexed read-model testnet evidence;
- production readiness or audit completion.

## Fixture labels

The case is labeled `COMPOSITE_FIXTURE`. Testnet evidence references are shown as provenance, but the app does not turn separately evidenced runs into a continuous run.

Local projection and workbench values are not canonical financial state. They are read-only observations and presentation data.
